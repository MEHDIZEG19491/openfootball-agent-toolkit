# Governance

OpenFootball Agent Toolkit is an independent, community-oriented project initiated
by Mahdi Zeghouani (MTRANSPRO). The initiating maintainer has final responsibility
for the initial repository, review, releases, security routing and continuity.
No additional maintainers, sponsors or adopting organizations are claimed.

The initial implementation was prepared with AI assistance. Human maintainers
must review and accept responsibility for a public release. AI tools do not vote,
provide independent approval or count as community contributors.

Routine changes use issues and pull requests. Substantial changes to storage,
authentication, public schemas, licensing or governance require a written proposal
and a reasonable opportunity for public feedback (normally at least seven days).
Urgent security fixes may use a confidential process followed by disclosure.

Maintainer access is earned through sustained, verifiable review and contributions,
not purchased engagement or affiliation. Before adding access, agree scope,
security practices, expected availability and a removal/rotation process. Grant
the least repository privilege needed. A contributor may remain independent of
all agencies and clubs.

Resolve technical disputes by comparing evidence, tests and user needs. The
primary maintainer records a decision and rationale when consensus is unavailable.
People must recuse themselves from decisions involving personal conflicts.
There is no steering committee until real participation supports one.

Releases use semantic versioning; prerelease schemas may change only with a
migration note. Never silently modify published tags. Maintain a change log and
publish known limitations. If maintenance stops, say so and identify succession
options; no service or support availability is guaranteed.

Project decisions prioritize privacy, interoperability and documented behavior.
Commercial use is permitted under the license, but does not imply project
endorsement. Any future sponsorship must be disclosed and must not buy private
data access, false testimonials or control over compliance conclusions.
