# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect across countries, languages, backgrounds,
identities and levels of technical experience. Focus criticism on the work.

We welcome clear questions, constructive disagreement, translation improvements,
credit for others' work and willingness to correct mistakes. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexual harassment, doxxing, private-data disclosure, impersonation
and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not pressure people to supply
private player records or promote a particular agency to participate.

This policy covers repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project-managed
channels and conduct when officially representing this project.

Before opening public community channels, the primary maintainer must publish a
private conduct-reporting route in the repository's contact settings. Until then,
use an existing private contact with the maintainer. Do not put sensitive reports
in public issues. Security reports follow SECURITY.md.

Maintainers assess reports confidentially as far as practical, hear relevant
perspectives and disclose only what is necessary. Responses may include a private
warning, removal of content, temporary limits or exclusion for serious/repeated
harm. A maintainer who is involved must recuse themselves; if no independent
maintainer is available, seek an agreed external moderator before deciding an
appeal. Appeals should identify new evidence or a process error. No retaliation.

No enforcement team or response-time commitment is claimed at project launch.
